Today we are thrilled to announce the public release of **krantho**, a full-site content framework designed for modern web development.

## Why Krantho?

Traditional CMS platforms force you to choose between developer experience and content editor experience. Krantho bridges this gap with a declarative content model that works across frameworks.

## Key Features

- **Declarative content model** — define your content structure with `defineCollection()` and Zod schemas
- **Page composition** — `definePage()` declares what content a page needs, `resolvePageContent()` resolves it
- **Framework agnostic** — works with Next.js, SvelteKit, Vite SSR, or pure SPA
- **Agentic SEO** — AI-powered content enrichment using Claude CLI

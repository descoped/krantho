## What We Do

Our strategy practice helps leadership teams make high-stakes decisions with confidence. We combine rigorous analysis with practical market insight to build roadmaps that are actionable — not theoretical.

## Typical Engagements

- Market entry and expansion strategy
- Competitive analysis and positioning
- Growth planning and OKR design
- M&A due diligence (commercial)

## Who It's For

CEOs, founders, and executive teams at companies with $5M-$500M in revenue who are navigating a critical transition — new market, new product line, or post-funding scale.

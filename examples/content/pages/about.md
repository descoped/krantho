### What we look for

We hire people who combine *analytical rigor* with ~~theoretical thinking~~ practical execution. Every consultant at Acme has:

1. **Deep domain expertise** in at least one industry vertical
2. **Hands-on delivery experience** — not just advisory
3. **Strong communication skills** — our work lives and dies by clarity

We also value `T-shaped` skills: deep in one area, broad across many.

## Our Approach

We believe in three principles:

1. **Start with the problem, not the solution.** We invest time upfront to understand the real challenge before proposing a path forward.
2. **Work alongside, not above.** Our consultants embed with your team. Knowledge transfer is built into every engagement.
3. **Measure what matters.** Every engagement has clear success criteria, defined collaboratively and tracked transparently.

### Our process

```
Discovery → Diagnosis → Design → Deliver → Debrief
   2 weeks     2 weeks    4 weeks   8-16 weeks   1 week
```

- **Discovery** — stakeholder interviews, data review, landscape scan
- **Diagnosis** — root cause analysis, opportunity mapping
- **Design** — solution architecture, implementation roadmap
- **Deliver** — hands-on execution alongside your team
- **Debrief** — retrospective, knowledge transfer, next steps

> **Note:** Every engagement includes a mid-point checkpoint where we reassess scope and priorities with the client team.

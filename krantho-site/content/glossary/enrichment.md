---
title: Enrichment
definition: Using a language model to draft what content is missing, such as meta descriptions or Schema.org properties, without overwriting what people wrote.
docs-page: seo-pipeline
status: published
---

Enrichment runs as a background job started from Studio, the API or the CLI, or as a build-time tool that previews its changes before applying them. It uses the provider and model configured under `llm`. Authors review what it drafts; values they have set are left alone.

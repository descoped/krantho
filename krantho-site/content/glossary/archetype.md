---
title: Archetype
definition: A Schema.org type assigned to content — a business type for the site, an Article or a Service for a collection — that decides its structured data.
docs-page: seo-pipeline
status: published
---

Archetypes are configured at four levels: the site, each collection, optional URL patterns, and single documents. The developer seeds them from the `seo` section of `krantho.config.ts`, and editors refine them in Studio. Krantho fills the properties it can derive from the content and leaves judgment to the author.

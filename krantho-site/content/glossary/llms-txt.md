---
title: llms.txt
definition: A plain-text file at the root of a site that tells AI assistants what the site is and links its main pages with short descriptions.
docs-page: seo-pipeline
status: published
---

The build-time SEO pipeline in `@krantho/tools` generates `llms.txt` from your business data and content. Its link descriptions can be written by enrichment and reviewed before they are applied. Your app serves the generated file from a route.

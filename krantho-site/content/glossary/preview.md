---
title: Preview
definition: Seeing a draft on the real page before it is published, shown in Studio's Live mode or opened with Open on site.
docs-page: studio
status: published
---

Visitors always see the published version; an editor in preview sees the draft in the site's own layout. Next.js sites use draft mode, and SvelteKit and Express sites use a signed link that is valid for ten minutes and for one path only. Preview is offered when the site enables it.

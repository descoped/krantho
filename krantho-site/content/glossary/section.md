---
title: Section
definition: 'The part of a document body under one ## heading, available to page templates by its heading text.'
docs-page: content-fields
status: published
---

Krantho splits a Markdown body at its `##` headings and renders each part on its own. A page template can place each section where its design wants it, and give some sections a designed treatment while the rest render as prose. Sections keep long-form content editable as one body.

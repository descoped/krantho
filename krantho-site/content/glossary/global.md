---
title: Global
definition: A document of the globals collection, holding site-wide content such as navigation, footer or an announcement.
docs-page: content-model
status: published
---

Globals are read by the layout rather than shown as pages of their own, so they are left out of the sitemap and have no page to preview. Editors change them in Studio like any other document. A published change to a global marks every cached page that reads it as stale, and each renders again when it is next visited.

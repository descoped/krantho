---
title: Frontmatter
definition: "The YAML block between two --- lines at the top of a Markdown file, holding the document's fields."
docs-page: content-fields
status: published
---

Frontmatter is how fields are written in content files. When krantho imports a file, the frontmatter becomes the document's fields and the rest of the file becomes its body. The same data is edited as a form in Studio.

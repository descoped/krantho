---
title: 'Is krantho a hosted service?'
topic: product
order: 2
docs-page: introduction
status: published
---

No. Krantho is a set of packages that runs inside your own app. Your app serves the API at `/api` and Studio at `/studio`, your content lives in your database, and your media in the store you choose. There is no krantho account and no krantho server to sign up for. You host the site, as you would without a CMS.

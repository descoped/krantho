This is the first release of krantho, and where its changelog starts. Krantho is a CMS library: you add it to a site you build yourself, and it runs inside your app, on your host and your database.

## Added

- **Content templates in code.** A collection is a Zod schema in `krantho.collections.ts`. The schema is the form editors fill in, and your page templates render the documents. Content is markdown with frontmatter, kept in your repository and imported into the database on deploy.
- **Studio.** Editors work at `/studio`, served by your app on Next.js, SvelteKit and Express. They edit on the page itself in Live mode or in a form, save drafts, publish, and restore earlier versions. Studio also manages media, locales, people and the site's structured data.
- **Structured SEO.** Archetypes for the site, each collection, a route and a single document compose one JSON-LD graph per page. Meta tags, Open Graph, the sitemap, `robots.txt` and `llms.txt` come from the same configuration. A language model can fill missing meta descriptions and Schema.org properties, and never overwrites what an editor wrote.
- **Your database and files.** SQLite by default and Postgres in production. Media goes to local disk, Vercel Blob, an S3-compatible store or Google Cloud Storage.
- **Page caching.** A page is rendered once and kept until a change invalidates it: with Next.js ISR, or at a CDN (CloudFront, Cloud CDN or Vercel) for SvelteKit and Express.
- **A REST API** described by an OpenAPI 3.1 specification, with plugins, webhooks and sign-in through a token or OpenID Connect.
- **A command-line tool and agent skills** for working with content from a terminal or a coding agent.
- **`create-krantho`** scaffolds a new project.
